Clause 03

3. Installation & Electrical Safety

electrical_services
warning Important Electrical Installation Notice

All iRAV hardware requires commissioning by an accredited, licensed “Grade A” electrical contractor. Installation must adhere precisely to CEA Technical Standards for Connectivity of Distributed Generation, IEEE 1547, and National Electrical Code (NEC) Section 625.

  • Single-Phase Feeds (230V ± 10%): Dedicated 40A upstream miniature circuit breaker (MCB) with integrated 30mA Type A or Type B RCD is mandatory.
  • Three-Phase Feeds (415V ± 10%): Independent earth resistance must measure below 5 Ohms prior to unit commissioning.
  • Earthing Isolation: TN-S or TT earthing schemes must be structurally verified. Under no circumstances should PEN conductor bonding occur inside the charging pedestal.
