02 / Taxonomy of Collected Signals

What Data We Receive & Why

We adhere strictly to the principle of data minimization: we process only the digital signals necessary to ensure electrical safety, charge verification, grid stability, and seamless owner authentication.

badge

Identity & Credentials

When onboarding through the iRAV App or enterprise operator portal, we record your verified mobile phone number (for cryptographic OTP login), voluntary display name, company tax registration (for B2B fleet receipts), and active RFID UID tokens assigned to your key fob.

Zero third-party social trackers or advertising SDKs.
pin_drop

Location & Station Coordinates

Public charging station GPS pins are public to aid navigation. For private residential ARKIN units, geocoordinates are stored in salted encrypted hashes purely to synchronize regional grid tariffs and local sunrise/sunset timings for solar net-metering.

Residential coordinates are never indexed on public directories.

04 / Purpose & Processing Limits

Engineered For Energy, Not Advertising

iRAV builds sustainable hardware. We operate on a direct hardware purchase and software-as-a-service model, meaning our business model never relies on commercializing user habits or profiling travel corridors.

solar_power

Dynamic Solar & Time-of-Day Sync

We process instantaneous grid frequency and your local DISCOM tariff structures to trigger autonomous charging when renewable generation is peak or electricity rates are lowest.

system_update

Predictive Thermal OTA Updates

Aggregated thermals allow our engineers to push micro-firmware refinements to the switching power converters, preventing over-heating during peak 48°C Indian summer conditions.

block

What We Strictly Never Do

We never broker vehicle telematics to insurance firms without express opt-in consent, never insert advertising into the iRAV companion app, and never track your vehicle routes outside of charger connection timestamps.

05 / Enterprise Security & Infrastructure

Bank-Grade Cryptography for Clean Power

Every transaction, reservation, and firmware deployment is verified through a hardened mutual TLS pipeline adhering to global automotive benchmarks.

public Sovereign Data Hosting

All cloud telemetry databases are hosted strictly in AWS Mumbai (ap-south-1) with cold storage replicas in Hyderabad. Full compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023) of India.

verified_user Hardware Root of Trust

Each ARKIN motherboard features a discrete ATECC608 secure element crypto coprocessor. Private keys never leave hardware memory, neutralizing man-in-the-middle grid attacks.
